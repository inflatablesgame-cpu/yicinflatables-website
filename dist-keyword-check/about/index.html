Guangzhou, China · OEM / ODM inflatable production

Guangzhou Inflatable Manufacturer & OEM Factory

YIC Inflatable Sports is a custom inflatable manufacturer in China, serving party rental companies, entertainment venues, event operators, and distributors with factory-direct commercial inflatable production.

Who we are

A Factory Built for Commercial Inflatable Production and OEM Projects

YIC Inflatable Sports Co., Ltd. has more than 15 years of experience manufacturing and exporting commercial inflatable products from Guangzhou, China. Our team includes 116 long-term professionals, supported by automatic cutting, heavy-duty sewing, and heat-sealing equipment.

As a B2B inflatable party rental supplier, we work with more than 3,100 rental companies, entertainment businesses, event operators, and distributors across 57 countries, including the United States, the United Kingdom, and European markets.

Our OEM/ODM inflatable production service turns your reference image, dimensions, artwork, product brief, and market requirements into a practical factory specification for production, inspection, packing, and export.
