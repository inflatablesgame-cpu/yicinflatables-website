Buyer questions

Inflatable Mechanical Games FAQ

Practical answers for product selection, customization, site planning, and factory quotations.

What site information is needed for a mechanical attraction?

Confirm the footprint, access route, floor or surface, power supply, clearance, queue area, operator position, supervision, and intended operating environment.

Can the attraction be branded or themed?

Yes. Theme graphics, colors, printed panels, signage, and selected exterior details can be adapted after the operating requirements are confirmed.

Does the quotation include operator and safety planning?

YIC can document the product specification, accessories, inspection points, and operating considerations; local operators remain responsible for venue procedures and applicable requirements.

What material and construction details should I confirm for commercial inflatable mechanical games?

Confirm the product-specific material, reinforcement, seams, accessories, dimensions, intended use, and destination-market requirements with YIC before production.

How do I request a factory quotation for inflatable mechanical games?

Send the model or reference image, quantity, application, destination, preferred dimensions, artwork, and target delivery date so YIC can prepare a clear quotation.

Which power details should I confirm for an inflatable mechanical game?

Provide the destination voltage, frequency, plug type, and venue power arrangement so the equipment and electrical details can be checked for the requested model.
