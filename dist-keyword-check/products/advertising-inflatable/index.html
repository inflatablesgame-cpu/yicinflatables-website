Buyer questions

Advertising Inflatable FAQ

Practical answers for product selection, customization, site planning, and factory quotations.

Can the inflatable be made to our exact brand shape?

Yes. YIC can review a reference image or concept and develop the outline, dimensions, colors, printed artwork, entrances, display areas, and accessories for your campaign.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Share the artwork, intended venue, viewing direction, footprint, quantity, destination, and target event date so the factory can confirm a practical specification.

Are advertising inflatables suitable for repeated events?

They can be planned for repeated commercial use with appropriate material, reinforcement, packing, storage, anchoring, and operating instructions.

What material and construction details should I confirm for commercial advertising inflatable?

Confirm the product-specific material, reinforcement, seams, accessories, dimensions, intended use, and destination-market requirements with YIC before production.

How do I request a factory quotation for advertising inflatable?

Send the model or reference image, quantity, application, destination, preferred dimensions, artwork, and target delivery date so YIC can prepare a clear quotation.

Can advertising inflatables be reused across multiple campaigns?

They can be planned for repeat events. Confirm the display structure, replaceable or fixed artwork, packing, storage, anchoring, and inspection needs for the campaign schedule.
