Buyer questions

Commercial Inflatable Product FAQ

Practical answers for selecting products, planning a mixed order, and starting an OEM or wholesale conversation.

What types of commercial inflatables does YIC manufacture?

YIC supplies bounce houses, bounce house combos, slides, obstacle courses, inflatable games, water attractions, theme parks, advertising inflatables, tents, and other custom commercial formats for business use.

Which products are suitable for party rental businesses?

Bounce houses, combos, dry slides, obstacle courses, and interactive games are common rental-fleet choices. The right mix depends on your customer demand, venue footprint, transport plan, setup time, and expected repeat use.

Can I order mixed inflatable products in one shipment?

Mixed product orders can be discussed. Send the models, quantities, destination, preferred delivery timing, and any shared artwork or accessory requirements so YIC can review packing and quotation options.

Can YIC customize a product that is not shown in the catalog?

Yes. Share a reference image, sketch, dimensions, colors, artwork, play features, intended application, quantity, and destination. The team can review a custom OEM or ODM design before production.

What information is needed for a commercial inflatable quotation?

Include the product or reference image, quantity, destination country and city, preferred dimensions, application, artwork or logo, target delivery date, accessories, and any required documents.

Does YIC support distributors and importers?

Yes. YIC works with distributors and importers on product selection, private-label artwork, repeatable specifications, mixed product ranges, packing details, and export coordination.
