Choose the participant group

Buyer questions

Bumper Ball FAQ

Practical answers for product selection, customization, site planning, and factory quotations.

How many bumper balls should I order?

The quantity depends on the activity format, participant count, session size, storage capacity, and whether the set is for rentals, schools, or an operator-led venue.

Can the bumper balls carry team colors or a logo?

Yes. Color combinations and suitable logo or graphic placement can be reviewed as part of the production brief.

What surface is suitable for bubble soccer?

Use a clear, suitable playing surface with enough run-off and supervision space. Confirm the venue surface and operating rules before ordering.

What material and construction details should I confirm for commercial bumper ball?

Confirm the product-specific material, reinforcement, seams, accessories, dimensions, intended use, and destination-market requirements with YIC before production.

How do I request a factory quotation for bumper ball?

Send the model or reference image, quantity, application, destination, preferred dimensions, artwork, and target delivery date so YIC can prepare a clear quotation.

How do I choose bumper ball sizes for my participants?

Share the participant age range, activity format, and quantity. YIC can review suitable ball dimensions and set configuration for the planned group use.
