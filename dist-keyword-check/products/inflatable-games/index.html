How to choose commercial inflatable games

Use these buying criteria to shortlist a practical format before comparing individual models.

01

Choose the game objective

Select target, sports, competitive, team, or carnival play according to the event and audience.

02

Plan player throughput

03

Check venue requirements

Confirm footprint, height, access, floor protection, blower or power needs, and indoor or outdoor use.

04

Customize the experience

Adapt targets, lanes, rules, artwork, branding, colors, and accessories to the program.

Buyer questions

Inflatable Games FAQ

Practical answers for product selection, customization, site planning, and factory quotations.

Which inflatable game is right for a rental fleet?

Choose according to the player age, venue footprint, setup time, repeat-play value, supervision plan, and whether the attraction is a target, sports, or competitive game.

Can the game rules or target layout be customized?

Yes. The play objective, target positions, colors, graphics, dimensions, and branded instructions can be reviewed for an OEM or ODM design.

Are inflatable games suitable for indoor venues?

Many formats can be adapted for indoor use when the height, access route, floor protection, blower position, ventilation, and local venue rules are confirmed.

What material and construction details should I confirm for commercial inflatable games?

Confirm the product-specific material, reinforcement, seams, accessories, dimensions, intended use, and destination-market requirements with YIC before production.

How do I request a factory quotation for inflatable games?

Send the model or reference image, quantity, application, destination, preferred dimensions, artwork, and target delivery date so YIC can prepare a clear quotation.

Can I order several inflatable game formats together?

Yes. Send the game list, quantities, event or venue plan, destination, and timing so YIC can review a combined quotation, production schedule, and packing arrangement.
