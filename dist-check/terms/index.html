Working with the factory

Website Terms

Important conditions for using the YIC catalog, requesting quotations, and discussing custom commercial inflatable projects.

Catalog information

Product photos, reference dimensions, materials, accessories, and applications are provided to help buyers shortlist a model. The final specification is the approved quotation and production document for the requested product and destination market.

Custom projects and quotations

Custom color, artwork, dimensions, layouts, accessories, lead time, packing, shipping terms, payment terms, and applicable documents must be confirmed in writing before production. A website enquiry is a request for discussion, not an accepted purchase order.

Compliance and operation

Certificates and test reports are product-, component-, and market-specific. Buyers are responsible for confirming local installation, supervision, anchoring, electrical, insurance, and operating requirements before using an attraction.

Questions

For clarification about a quotation or product specification, contact lanna@yicinflatables.com.
