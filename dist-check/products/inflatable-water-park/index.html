Buyer questions

Inflatable Water Park FAQ

Practical answers for product selection, customization, site planning, and factory quotations.

What site details are essential for a floating water park?

Confirm water depth, area, access, anchoring points, expected capacity, weather and season, rescue or supervision plan, storage, and local operating requirements.

Can the water park modules be arranged to fit our site?

Yes. YIC can review the water area and create a modular arrangement with custom colors, branding, obstacle features, and project dimensions.

How should a water attraction be stored between seasons?

Confirm cleaning, drying, deflation, repair, packed volume, protection, and storage conditions as part of the product and operating plan.

What material and construction details should I confirm for commercial inflatable water park?

Confirm the product-specific material, reinforcement, seams, accessories, dimensions, intended use, and destination-market requirements with YIC before production.

How do I request a factory quotation for inflatable water park?

Send the model or reference image, quantity, application, destination, preferred dimensions, artwork, and target delivery date so YIC can prepare a clear quotation.

What information helps plan a floating water park layout?

Share a water-area plan, depth information, access points, anchoring locations, target capacity, module preferences, operating season, and supervision arrangements.
