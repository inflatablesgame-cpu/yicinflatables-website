Production process

Commercial Inflatable Manufacturing & Quality Control

From your approved design to export packing, defined checkpoints keep materials, reinforced workmanship, inflation performance, and shipment readiness aligned before your order leaves the factory.

01

Requirements, design & approval

We confirm dimensions, layout, artwork, and key product details with you before production begins.

02

Material and cutting

Materials are checked and cut against the approved production layout.

03

Sewing and reinforcement

Seams and high-stress points are built for repeated commercial use.

04

Heat sealing

Selected product structures receive controlled heat-sealed splicing.

05

Inflation testing

Finished units are inflated to check appearance, seams, and operation.

06

Final inspection

Inspection points are completed before the product is packed for export.

07

Export packing

Packing and shipment details are prepared for your destination market.

YIC Manufacturer & Factory FAQ

What kind of manufacturer is YIC Inflatable Sports?

YIC Inflatable Sports is a commercial inflatable manufacturer serving rental companies, family entertainment centers, resorts, event operators, and distributors with factory-direct production and export support.

How long has YIC manufactured commercial inflatables?

YIC has more than 15 years of manufacturing and export experience, with 116 long-term production professionals and customers in 57 countries.

Does YIC provide factory-direct OEM and ODM manufacturing?

Yes. Buyers can bring an existing model or share a concept, reference image, dimensions, artwork, and intended use for an OEM or ODM project discussion with the factory.

How does YIC manage production quality?

Quality checkpoints can include material review, cutting, sewing and reinforcement, heat sealing where applicable, inflation testing, final inspection, and export packing.

Can YIC confirm product documents for my destination market?

YIC reviews documentation against the specific product, components, and destination-market requirements. Certificate scope is checked for the requested model rather than assumed to apply universally.

Does YIC support international B2B customers?

Yes. YIC supports international buyers with factory communication, production coordination, export packing, and shipment information for their destination.
