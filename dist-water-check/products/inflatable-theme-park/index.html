Buyer questions

Inflatable Theme Park FAQ

Practical answers for product selection, customization, site planning, and factory quotations.

What information is needed for a theme park project quote?

Share the venue footprint, ceiling or height limits, target age, expected capacity, theme references, modules, destination, installation plan, and target opening date.

Can YIC create a complete custom themed layout?

Yes. The factory can coordinate concept references, 3D layout, artwork approval, module arrangement, dimensions, production, and project documentation.

How should guest flow and supervision be planned?

Plan visible entry and exit points, age-appropriate zones, queue and spectator areas, operator access, emergency routes, and local venue procedures.

What material and construction details should I confirm for commercial inflatable theme park?

Confirm the product-specific material, reinforcement, seams, accessories, dimensions, intended use, and destination-market requirements with YIC before production.

How do I request a factory quotation for inflatable theme park?

Send the model or reference image, quantity, application, destination, preferred dimensions, artwork, and target delivery date so YIC can prepare a clear quotation.

Can a themed inflatable park be planned in phases?

A project can be discussed around priority zones, future expansion, available site area, guest flow, installation timing, and a consistent theme and artwork brief.
